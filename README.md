# AI Fintech Visibility Check

A practical checklist for testing whether European fintech websites are easy for AI systems to retrieve, understand and cite.

## Why this exists

AI systems do not always retrieve financial information in the same way users do.

This checklist focuses on common technical and entity level problems that can make important fintech information difficult to retrieve or interpret.

## Checklist

### Crawlability

- [ ] Important pages are indexable
- [ ] Important content is available in HTML
- [ ] Important content does not depend entirely on JavaScript

### Entity clarity

- [ ] Company name is consistent
- [ ] Company role is clearly explained
- [ ] Products and services are clearly identified
- [ ] Regulatory information is clearly stated

### Structured data

- [ ] Relevant structured data is present
- [ ] Structured data describes the correct entity
- [ ] Important relationships are represented clearly

### Internal linking

- [ ] Important regulatory pages are internally linked
- [ ] Product pages connect to relevant company information
- [ ] Important pages are easy to discover from other pages

### AI retrieval

- [ ] An external AI system can identify the company
- [ ] An external AI system can understand what the company does
- [ ] An external AI system can identify the relevant jurisdiction
- [ ] An external AI system can identify the relevant regulatory information
